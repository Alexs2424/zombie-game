A warm, weathered and authentic American voice with deep gravel and heart. Perfect for gritty storytelling, old-timer commercials, historical documentaries, and character roles that need wisdom and texture.

Tatiana - Smoky, Husky and Intriguing

Rosie - Smoky, Warm, and Subtle

Rose is a captivating female narrator with the rich, smoky resonance of a bygone era. Her tone is velvety and low, laced with a subtle mystery that draws listeners into the past. Whether she's recounting tales of lost love, unraveling noir mysteries, or delivering monologues that echo from an old radio, Rose embodies timeless elegance. Her voice is slow, sultry, and deliberate — the kind that belongs in a dimly lit jazz lounge or a vintage newsreel.
