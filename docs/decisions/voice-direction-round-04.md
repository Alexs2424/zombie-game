# Original voice redesign — round 04

## Established direction and scope

On 2026-09-30 the user confirmed adding ElevenLabs API credits, requested redesigning **all** voices to make them more unique, and said the lines still need to be more theatrical. Voice identity remains the priority. The earlier preference for Chuck Miller remains a positive texture reference; it does not exempt Frankie from this newly requested original redesign.

This round supersedes round 03's plan to record library candidates first. All six characters receive original text-described Voice Design auditions. No existing person's recording or library voice is used as a cloning reference. Names, biographies, ethnicities and regional backgrounds remain intact. Catalog descriptions are no longer the casting deliverable: the deliverable is new audio of our own scripts.

## Current working designs

| Character | Distinctive vocal construction | Theatrical behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frankie | Thick, coarse chest baritone plus a crooked nasal edge; musical North Jersey vowels. | Affectionate appetite for a gun becomes huge generosity toward a doomed crowd, then the swagger vanishes during guilt. |
| Eve | Unusually low smoky contralto, rich chest resonance and razor-bright diction; London with south London roots. | Private sensual delight opens into grand stage authority; damage to her own work produces an untidy, explosive tantrum. |
| Leon | Warm sandy middle-low resonance with a forward Texas twang; not another gangster bass. | A delighted, almost musical rising complaint contrasts with a big laugh and unembarrassed tenderness about home. |
| Vivian | Mobile low-middle voice, smoky bottom and a bright nasal sting; ragged laugh, native Los Angeles English. | Fast conspiratorial asides, languid greed and a sudden high snap of absurd outrage. Her sales rhythm disappears when ashamed. |
| Voss | Rounded bass-baritone with a fine dry burr and smiling upper overtones; cultivated American / light mid-Atlantic diction. | Almost operatic hospitality becomes an intimate demand; refusal provokes genuine outrage before the host forcibly returns. |
| Marlowe | Narrow weathered middle-register tenor, paper rasp and a faint reedy breath; northern English/Yorkshire vowels. | Courteous understatement can break into a startling piercing command, then settle back into manners. Shame removes the clever timing. |

Marlowe's tenor target replaces his prior middle-low brief to improve ensemble separation. His apparent age and supernatural status remain unresolved. Leon's twang is cadence and resonance, not a new cowboy biography. Theatrical does not mean everyone shouts continuously; each audition deliberately contains a quieter human turn.

## Exact audition script

The following text is a performance test, not an approved gameplay bank. Each character's three beats are joined into one preview script so all returned identities perform the same material. Whole audition clips must not be wired into short combat triggers. Longer lines belong to acting tests or suitably quiet authored scenes.

### Frankie · A dangerous man having far too much fun

**frankie.r04.01 — Acquiring a double-barrel shotgun**

> Ohhh, sweetheart. Where have you been all my life? Look at those barrels! Ruth would have HATED you.

Delivery: Greedy, intimate admiration builds into delighted, cracked laughter.

**frankie.r04.02 — After a rapid multi-kill**

> Everybody gets a turn! I am a VERY generous man!

Delivery: Open-armed, outrageous hospitality; still out of breath.

**frankie.r04.03 — Quiet confession after the Keene evidence**

> He asked me to wait. I heard him. ... I left anyway.

Delivery: Drop all swagger. Plain, unprotected, almost reluctant.

### Eve · Velvet command, spectacular temper

**eve.r04.01 — First extravagant firearm acquisition**

> Oh, that is FILTHY. ... Give it here. A lady should always arrive with something devastating.

Delivery: Low private delight, then gloriously public self-importance.

**eve.r04.02 — Costume damage acting exercise; not an implemented mechanic**

> You tore my sleeve. I made that. I MADE THAT, you miserable sack of leftovers!

Delivery: Control collapses into a sudden theatrical outburst.

**eve.r04.03 — Future authenticated Art recording**

> No. Play it again. ... Please. I need to hear if it is him.

Delivery: Quiet, bare and shaky; stop performing for an audience.

### Leon · Warm thunder with a frayed edge

**leon.r04.01 — First firing of a newly acquired loud automatic**

> Well, would you LISTEN to her! Della, forgive me. I have met somebody loud, expensive, and absolutely no good for me.

Delivery: Warm astonishment opens into an enormous pleased laugh; mock confession.

**leon.r04.02 — Reload finishes just before killing an approaching enemy**

> Four seconds! I ask for FOUR SECONDS, and the whole damn cemetery gets ambitious!

Delivery: Exasperation builds upward; furious, precise and ridiculous. Timing is an acting scenario, not a runtime claim.

**leon.r04.03 — Quiet post-escape moment**

> I need a telephone. No, the building can wait. ... I am calling home.

Delivery: Firm, warm, ordinary. A decision, not another joke.

### Vivian · A smoky laugh and a very expensive temper

**vivian.r04.01 — Taking a firearm from a known private stash**

> Oh, he was SAVING this. For what? His second funeral? ... Come to Mama.

Delivery: Conspiratorial discovery; delighted disbelief, then greedy affection for the gun.

**vivian.r04.02 — A door costs more than her available chips**

> Eight hundred? For a DOOR? Darling, I charge less to ruin a marriage!

Delivery: Immediate spectacular outrage; an absurd boast, not a new biographical fact.

**vivian.r04.03 — Quiet Tomas admission**

> I keep sending money. He keeps sending it back. ... I do not know what else to send.

Delivery: Flat, unfinished, unable to sell a way out of the feeling.

### Voss · The host who enjoys owning the room

**voss.r04.01 — A threatening welcome after hostility is established**

> My dear, I paid for the flowers. I paid for the orchestra. I even paid the man who will carry you out. The LEAST you can do is enjoy the evening.

Delivery: Lavish hospitality grows joyfully extravagant, then lands as a quiet personal demand. Audition rhetoric, not new prop commitments.

**voss.r04.02 — A crew member refuses him**

> No? ... You came into MY house to say NO to ME? ... How refreshing.

Delivery: Genuine wounded outrage; visibly force the gracious host back into his voice.

**voss.r04.03 — Quiet Helena admission**

> I knew everything she liked. ... Apparently that was not the same as listening.

Delivery: For one sentence he is a disappointed man, not the owner.

### Marlowe · A ruined tenor with immaculate manners

**marlowe.r04.01 — A corpse collapses by the counter**

> Sir! ... If you are going to come apart, do it OVER THERE. ... That is the good rug.

Delivery: A sudden sharp command, outraged precision, then immaculate quiet manners. Staging test, not a rug gameplay rule.

**marlowe.r04.02 — After the crew understands the House bargain**

> Mr Voss says the drinks are on the house. Very generous. ... Considering what the house drinks.

Delivery: An ordinary service announcement develops a dry, dangerous implication; no horror-trailer voice.

**marlowe.r04.03 — Marlowe admits his initial silence**

> I hoped you would find a way out that did not involve me. ... There. Nothing mysterious about it. Just a coward.

Delivery: Stop being charming. Tired, ashamed, angry with himself.

## Script guardrails

Audition rhetoric does not silently expand canon. Vivian's marriage boast is not a newly verified past scheme. Voss's flowers, orchestra and hired carrier are performance rhetoric, not new required NPCs or props. The sleeve damage, precise four-second reload complaint and rug staging are acting scenarios, not implemented mechanics. References to Ruth, Della, Art, Tomas, Helena and Keene follow existing dossiers. No open fate is resolved by this script.

The multi-kill speech does not claim a grenade kill, specific body count or final enemy. Before splitting these into gameplay assets, bind each selected short line to a true event and retest duration, clarity, cooldown and interruption. Approval of a voice does not automatically approve every line.

## Generation and evaluation

Bounded batch: one Voice Design request per character using `eleven_ttv_v3`, guidance 5, loudness 0.5, no automatic text replacement/enhancement and no automatic retries. Frankie tests restored access first; the remaining five follow only after success. Each request is expected to return three candidates. This is audition generation, not permanent voice creation or automatic game replacement.

[Round 04 JSON](../voice-auditions/round-04.json) owns exact prompts, contexts, delivery and request scripts. `tools/voice-auditions/generate-round-04.py` skips completed characters and leaves a pending marker after an uncertain request. Never erase a marker just to rerun the batch. Raw provider responses stay under ignored local outputs; publish only playable previews and provenance, not encoded duplicate audio or credentials.

Choose by listening for recognizable identity, convincing age/accent, audible grain, amusement, abrupt changes of scale and the ability to become plain. Reject steady narrator pacing, continuous stock growling, pretty but empty breathiness, or a voice whose only distinction is pitch. Chuck's preview remains a comparison for grit, not a person to imitate. A/B/C are candidate labels, not quality rankings.

The existing rejected Adam pilot remains technically installed until a selected voice and short production takes are integrated. No four-player voice attribution or new quest implementation is implied by these auditions.
