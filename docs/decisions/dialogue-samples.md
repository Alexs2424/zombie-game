# Heightened cast — first tone samples

Status: historical first tone samples, superseded for current writing by [contextual dialogue](contextual-dialogue.md). Not final dialogue or generated audio. Character direction lives in [characters.md](characters.md). These are comic reactions, not new lore, rewards, purchasable services, or mechanics. A production pass must add full event metadata, knowledge conditions, replay limits, and mix priorities.

The expanded first audition batch is in [voice auditions, round 01](../voice-auditions/README.md): six comic lines and one serious range test per character, plus exact voice-generation prompts. This page preserves the initial tone samples; the audition pack owns the wording used for generation.

## Frankie

- **After being bitten; wounded indignation, then a roar:** “You bite me? After I wore a tie to your funeral?”
- **Reviving a teammate; insists this is a reprimand:** “You wanna see a bright light? Look at me. I'm furious.”

## Eve

- **A corpse rises; professionally insulted:** “Resurrection. On this carpet. Nobody here understands presentation.”
- **Escaping a surround; triumphant announcement, then a private admission:** “An exit worthy of a standing ovation. I had absolutely nothing planned.”

## Leon

- **A supposedly disconnected machine starts; builds to an accusation:** “I disconnected you PERSONALLY. This is insubordination.”
- **Restoring power; satisfied, then territorial:** “There. Let there be light. And nobody touch a damn thing.”

## Vivian

- **Being revived; breathless attempt to regain authority:** “Excellent. You've passed the interview. You're my bodyguard.”
- **Hearing a ghost; delighted appraisal:** “Invisible, walks through walls... sweetheart, you're wasted in hospitality.”

## Voss

- **Players enter the cashier area; sumptuous PA delivery:** “Everything you see is insured. Do try to die facing away from the paintings.”
- **A player objects to his demands; intimate courtesy:** “Of course you have a choice. I had both options prepared especially for you.”

## Marlowe

- **A severed hand lands on the bar; practical, faintly inconvenienced:** “Whoever this belongs to, you're at your limit.”
- **Blood on the counter; offers a precise distinction:** “The screaming is complimentary. The upholstery isn't.”
